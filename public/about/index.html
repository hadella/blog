About

This blog is a personal space for writing about topics I find interesting. Expect infrequent but substantive posts—quality over quantity, maybe once a month or so.

Topics

Things I write about:

  • Math and Science: Explorations, explanations, and interesting problems
  • Vintage Technology: Old audio equipment, video formats, retro computing
  • Atari Computers: Programming, hardware, and the 8-bit era
  • RC Car Racing: Kyosho cars, racing tips, and restoration projects
  • Other Interests: Whatever else catches my attention

About Me

I’m a software developer with a background in embedded systems and C programming, branching out into other areas. Outside of work, I enjoy collecting and restoring vintage tech, racing RC cars, and diving deep into historical topics.
